# NLP_PJN

Materials from the NLP (Natural Language Processing, PJN) course – Jupyter notebooks from the classes with solved assignments.

**Author:** Myroslav Natalchenko

## Classes

1. [01 – Tokenization](01/) – tokenization, vocabulary, padding and part-of-speech tagging
2. [02 – Word vectors](02/) – embeddings, cosine similarity, gensim and Flair
3. [03 – LSTM](03/) – LSTM networks: predicting next word and sentence
4. [04 – Transformers, part 1](04/) – attention mechanism: query, key, value, single-head and multi-head attention
5. [05 – Transformers, part 2](05/) – full architecture: positional encoding, encoder, decoder, masking and training a mini-Transformer
6. [06 – Tuning, LoRA](06/) – fine-tuning pretrained models and parameter-efficient training of distilgpt2 with LoRA
7. [07 – RAG](07/) – Retrieval-Augmented Generation: document chunking, sentence embeddings, FAISS index, retrieval and answer generation with GPT-2
8. [08 – Quantization](08/) – model quantization: GGUF with llama.cpp, bitsandbytes 4-bit (NF4/FP4) and 8-bit, dynamic quantization; comparing size, speed and text quality
