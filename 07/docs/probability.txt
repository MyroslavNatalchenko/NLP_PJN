Probability theory is the branch of mathematics that describes random events. The probability of an event is a number between 0 and 1. A probability of 0 means the event is impossible, and a probability of 1 means the event is certain. When all outcomes are equally likely, the probability of an event is the number of favourable outcomes divided by the number of all possible outcomes. For example, the probability of rolling a 6 on a fair die is 1/6, and the probability of getting heads when tossing a coin is 1/2. Two events are independent if the result of one does not affect the other. For independent events, the probability that both happen is the product of their probabilities, so the probability of getting two heads in a row is 1/2 * 1/2 = 1/4.
